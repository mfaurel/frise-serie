# S02: yearToPixel density algorithm

**Goal:** Implement the yearToPixel data-driven density algorithm: buildDensityZones computes a non-linear pixel mapping from show distribution per era (ARCHITECTURE.md lines 148-178), yearToPixel/pixelToYear provide exact forward and inverse mapping, all proven by a Vitest suite covering linear interpolation, era boundary transitions, BC dates, density caps, and inverse-mapping accuracy.
**Demo:** Vitest suite passes: yearToPixel(793, zones) returns correct pixel for Vikings start, pixelToYear inverts it, BC dates work

## Must-Haves

- yearToPixel and pixelToYear pass unit tests including BC dates, era boundaries, and density edge cases; buildDensityZones produces a correct non-linear mapping from show distribution (D002: bucketed by Show.narrativeYearStart); every zone's pxPerYear stays within MIN_PX_PER_YEAR/MAX_PX_PER_YEAR caps; pixelToYear(yearToPixel(year, zones), zones) === year across a representative year sample spanning every era.

## Proof Level

- This slice proves: contract — pure functions proven by TypeScript compilation and Vitest assertions against real data/shows.ts and data/eras.ts; no runtime UI rendering required at this proof level
