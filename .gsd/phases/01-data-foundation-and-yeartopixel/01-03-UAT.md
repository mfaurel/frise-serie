## Edge Cases

### Year 0 century label

1. Call `yearToDisplay(0, "fr")` and `yearToDisplay(0, "en")` (covered in tests/year-to-display.test.ts).
2. **Expected:** Returns the special-cased century label ("Ier s." / "1st c.") rather than "0 av. J.-C." or "0 AD".

### Empty filter criteria

1. Call `filterShows(shows, {})` or equivalent empty-criteria call (covered in tests/filters.test.ts).
2. **Expected:** Returns all shows unfiltered — empty criteria in a category means "no filter" for that category, not "match nothing".

## Failure Signals

- Any regression in tests/year-to-pixel.test.ts or tests/data-integrity.test.ts (would indicate an unintended cross-slice break).
- Non-zero exit from `npx tsc --noEmit`.
